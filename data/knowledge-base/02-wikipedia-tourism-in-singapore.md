# Tourism in Singapore (Wikipedia)

## Visitor Context

Singapore attracted nearly 19.1 million visitors in 2019, the highest recorded number since
independence in 1965. Singapore is ranked the 5th most visited city in the world and 2nd in
the Asia-Pacific region.

## Iconic Landmarks (Marina Bay area)

The Marina Bay area features the Merlion, Gardens by the Bay, the Singapore Flyer, and
Marina Bay Sands — these prominently define the city's skyline and are symbols of its modern
architectural and cultural identity.

## Entertainment & Leisure (good for families)

- **Universal Studios Singapore** (Sentosa Island) — theme park, indoor/outdoor rides.
- **Jewel Changi Airport** — shopping and the Rain Vortex indoor waterfall (indoor, all
  weather, family-friendly).
- **Skyline Luge Singapore** (Sentosa) — outdoor go-kart-style ride.
- **Singapore Oceanarium** — large indoor aquarium (reopened July 2025 after expansion),
  ideal for a rainy day or with young children.

## Nature & Wildlife

Four major zoos/wildlife parks operate in the Mandai area:
- **Singapore Zoo** — outdoor, family-friendly.
- **Night Safari** — the world's first nocturnal zoo (outdoor, evening).
- **Bird Paradise** — features around 1,000 flamingos (outdoor).
- **River Wonders** — home to giant pandas (mix indoor/outdoor).

## Botanical & Green Spaces

- **Singapore Botanic Gardens** — UNESCO World Heritage Site with 3,000+ orchid varieties
  (outdoor, free entry to most areas).
- **Gardens by the Bay** — Flower Dome and Cloud Forest are indoor climate-controlled
  conservatories (good rainy-day option); Supertree Grove is outdoor.
- **Bukit Timah Nature Reserve** — primary rainforest, hiking trails (outdoor).
- **Sungei Buloh Wetland Reserve** — wetland nature reserve (outdoor).
- **Pulau Ubin** — rural island with traditional villages and nature trails (outdoor).

## Cultural & Historical Landmarks

- Masjid Sultan (1826), Thian Hock Keng Temple (1839), Sri Mariamman Temple (1827 — the
  oldest Hindu temple in Singapore), Kranji War Memorial, Civilian War Memorial.

### Museums (indoor, good for rainy days)
Asian Civilisations Museum, Peranakan Museum, National Museum of Singapore (the oldest,
dating to 1849), Singapore Art Museum, Changi Museum.

## Shopping & Commercial Precincts

**Orchard Road** is Singapore's premier shopping destination with numerous department
stores, malls, restaurants and coffeehouses. **ION Orchard** is a notable luxury shopping
centre (indoor, all-weather).

## Ethnic Quarters (cultural neighbourhoods for tourists)

- **Chinatown**
- **Geylang Serai** (Malay heritage)
- **Kampong Glam** (Islamic culture)
- **Little India**

## Dining & Culinary Tourism

Singapore's hawker culture was inscribed on UNESCO's Intangible Cultural Heritage list in
2020. Notable hawker centres: Lau Pa Sat, Newton Food Centre, Maxwell Food Centre. Signature
dishes include chilli crab, Hainanese chicken rice, fish head curry and laksa.

## Recent Developments (2023–2025)

Bird Paradise opened May 2023; Rainforest Wild Asia launched March 2025; Mandai Rainforest
Resort began operations April 2025; Universal Studios introduced Minion Land in February
2025.
