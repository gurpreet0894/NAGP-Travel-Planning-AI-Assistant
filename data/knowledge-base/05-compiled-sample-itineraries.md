## Sample 3-Day Sightseeing Itinerary

**Day 1 — Marina Bay & Civic District (mostly outdoor, some indoor)**
- Morning: Merlion Park, Marina Bay waterfront promenade.
- Midday: Asian Civilisations Museum or National Museum of Singapore (indoor).
- Afternoon: Gardens by the Bay — Supertree Grove (outdoor); Flower Dome / Cloud Forest
  (indoor, good if it rains).
- Evening: Marina Bay Sands observation deck, Spectra light show.

**Day 2 — Culture & Neighbourhoods (mixed indoor/outdoor)**
- Morning: Chinatown — Thian Hock Keng Temple, Sri Mariamman Temple, heritage shophouses.
- Midday: Lunch at Maxwell Food Centre (hawker food).
- Afternoon: Kampong Glam (Masjid Sultan) and Little India (Sri Srinivasa Perumal Temple).
- Evening: Clarke Quay / Boat Quay riverside dining.

**Day 3 — Sentosa & Family Fun (mostly outdoor, some indoor)**
- Morning: Sentosa Island — beaches or Skyline Luge (outdoor).
- Midday: Universal Studios Singapore (mixed indoor/outdoor rides).
- Afternoon: Singapore Oceanarium or Jewel Changi Airport Rain Vortex (indoor, all-weather
  backup).

## Family-with-Children Suggestions

Indoor-friendly and family-paced options: Singapore Oceanarium, Jewel Changi Airport (Rain
Vortex + play areas), Gardens by the Bay's Flower Dome/Cloud Forest, River Wonders (giant
pandas), National Museum of Singapore. Outdoor options: Singapore Zoo, Night Safari, Bird
Paradise, East Coast Park beaches, Sentosa beaches.

## Cultural-Focus Itinerary

Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Glam/Geylang Serai and Katong together cover Chinese,
Indian, Malay and Peranakan heritage. Combine with the Asian Civilisations Museum, Peranakan
Museum and Indian Heritage Centre for a deeper cultural itinerary. All of these neighbourhoods
are reachable by MRT.

## Indoor Activity Options (useful when rain is forecast)

- Gardens by the Bay — Flower Dome & Cloud Forest (conservatories)
- Singapore Oceanarium
- ION Orchard and other Orchard Road malls
- National Museum of Singapore, Asian Civilisations Museum, Peranakan Museum, Singapore Art
  Museum, Changi Museum
- Jewel Changi Airport
- SnowCity indoor snow centre
- Marina Bay Sands (casino, museum, shopping)

## Outdoor Activity Options (best in clear weather)

- Gardens by the Bay — Supertree Grove
- Singapore Botanic Gardens
- Singapore Zoo, Night Safari, Bird Paradise
- Southern Ridges treetop walk
- Pulau Ubin nature trails
- Sentosa beaches, Skyline Luge
- East Coast Park

## Notes on Weather-Adaptive Planning

Singapore has a tropical climate with near-daily rain, so itineraries should always keep an
indoor alternative in mind for outdoor-heavy days. When the AI assistant is asked to
"weather-adjust" an itinerary, it should pair each outdoor activity above with a matching
indoor alternative from the same neighbourhood or precinct where possible, and confirm the
actual forecast using the MCP weather tool rather than assuming typical climate patterns.
